These General Terms of Sale ("GTS") govern all luxury concierge services provided by Gorex Group SA ("GOREX LUXURY CONCIERGE") to its customers ("the Customer"). Any order implies unreserved acceptance of these GTS.

1. Purpose and scope

The GTS apply to the concierge, executive assistance, travel, lifestyle and security services offered by GOREX LUXURY CONCIERGE. They prevail over any other document of the Customer, unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing.

2. Quotations and formation of the contract

Each service is the subject of a quotation specifying its nature, price and terms. The contract is formed by acceptance of the quotation (signature or electronic validation) and, where applicable, by payment of the deposit. Urgent requests may be confirmed in writing (email) to ensure traceability.

3. Prices and pricing

Prices are expressed in euros. The VAT regime is determined by the status of the Customer:

  • Private customer (Belgium): Belgian VAT at the applicable rate.
  • Belgian business customer: reverse charge (Article 51 §2.4 of the Belgian VAT Code).
  • Intra-EU business customer: VAT exemption (Article 39bis of the Belgian VAT Code), subject to a valid intra-EU VAT number.
  • Customer outside the European Union: service outside the scope.

Third-party provider costs (hotels, carriers, ticketing) are re-invoiced to the Customer under the agreed terms.

Services depend on the availability of partner suppliers. GOREX LUXURY CONCIERGE endeavours to respond to requests as promptly as possible, without any guarantee of availability. Any modification requested by the Customer may result in a price adjustment and is subject to the written acceptance of GOREX LUXURY CONCIERGE.

5. Payment

Unless otherwise agreed, invoices are payable within 30 days of their issue date, by transfer to the professional bank account indicated. Urgent or high-value services may require advance payment. A structured communication (OGM/VCS) may be mentioned on the invoice to facilitate payment reconciliation.

Any late payment automatically entails, without prior notice, late-payment interest at the increased Belgian legal rate, as well as a flat-rate compensation of €40 for recovery costs (Article 6.6 of the Belgian Code of Economic Law). Unpaid invoices are subject to reminders and, where applicable, formal notice.

For business customers established in Belgium, the invoice may be issued and transmitted electronically via the Peppol network, in UBL 2.1 format (Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 / EN 16931 standard), in accordance with Belgian electronic invoicing regulations.

Cancellation conditions are specified per service. Failing that:

  • Cancellation more than 7 days in advance: refund of amounts paid, excluding non-recoverable costs incurred.

Non-refundable costs incurred with third-party providers remain due by the Customer.
